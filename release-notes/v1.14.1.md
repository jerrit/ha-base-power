## Base Power 1.14.1

Battery Charging now follows the battery's live power.

### Fixes
- **Battery Charging stayed off during top-ups.** A full battery drifts to 99% every few hours, then charges back to 100% for 20 to 30 minutes. The sensor ignored that, because a rise from 99 to 100 looked like rounding. It now turns on whenever **Battery Power** shows power going into the battery (0.2 kW or more), even at 99 or 100%. It turns off as soon as that stops.
- When Base sends no power reading, the sensor works as it did in 1.14.0, from the charge percent.

### Correction to the 1.14.0 notes
1.14.0 listed "Battery Power has stayed at 0 while charging" as a known issue. That was wrong. Battery Power goes negative while charging, about -0.2 to -1.0 kW during a top-up. A very slow charge can still read 0, because Base reports power in 0.1 kW steps.

Restart Home Assistant after updating.
