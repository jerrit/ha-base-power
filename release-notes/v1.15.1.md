## Base Power 1.15.1

Battery Level on grid is more accurate and updates every minute.

### Fixes
- **Battery Level read 3 to 4 points high** and capped at 100% near full. Base recalculated its 750 W backup time after the API change, so the 10% reserve in the 1.15.0 estimate no longer fit. The default is now 6.4%, from tchris227's outage test from 98% down to 50% (#29).
- **Battery Level lagged during a refill and jumped when the grid came back.** It preferred the daily overview, which refreshes about hourly, and showed about 52% while the battery was near 72%. At grid restore it swapped back to an old sample, jumped about 4 points, and briefly turned Battery Charging on. It now follows the live snapshot every minute, with the overview only as a fallback (#29).

### New
- **The reserve is learned per install.** During an outage Base sends the exact percent, and the integration works out your battery's reserve from it. It's saved and used once it has 10 readings, and shows in diagnostics under `reserve_estimate`. Until then the 6.4% default is used.

### Notes
- After a full charge, Battery Level can settle a little under 100% (about 98.4% on a Base Core).
- Base's backup hours don't seem to account for discharge losses, so real runtime is probably about 8% shorter than Backup Time shows.

### Thanks
Big thanks to tchris227, who switched off their main breaker to run a real outage test, logged Base's exact percent against the backup time from 98% down to 50%, and worked out the fix. This release, and much of 1.15.0, came from that testing.

Restart Home Assistant after updating.
